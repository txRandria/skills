# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or professional role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, documentation, and evidence
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels
- Assume good intent while discussing security-sensitive topics
- Respect responsible disclosure practices

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or intimidation
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing exploits, credentials, or harmful instructions irresponsibly
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project spaces

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile: [@txRandria](https://github.com/txRandria). Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.
